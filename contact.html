FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my request be recorded?

Yes. The form is connected to the SAP Kicker Formspree endpoint, so submitted form data is recorded in the Formspree account. Formspree can also send the submission to the configured target email.

Can I reply to the person who submitted the request?

Yes. The form uses the submitter's email field, which Formspree uses as the Reply-To address for email notifications.

What should I not include?

Do not submit passwords, credentials, private keys, customer information, confidential company data, or proprietary source code.

Do you provide one-to-one mentoring?

Yes. You can select 1:1 Mentoring in the request type and describe what you need.
